COMPANY HR POLICY HANDBOOK

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Unused leave can be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days; any leave beyond this cap will be forfeited at year end. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 business days in advance for approval by a direct manager. Sick leave is separate from annual leave and provides up to 30 days per year with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. The company operates a flexible working policy allowing employees to shift their start time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided they complete 8 working hours per day. Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval, and must be logged in the attendance system.

SECTION 3: PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION
New employees undergo a probationary period of 90 days from their start date. During probation, either the employee or the company may terminate employment with 7 days' written notice. Performance reviews are conducted at day 30 and day 75 of probation. Upon successful completion, employees receive written confirmation of permanent employment status.

SECTION 4: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Employees may claim reimbursement for business-related expenses including travel, client meals, and approved software subscriptions. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, accompanied by a valid receipt or invoice. Claims exceeding 5,000 THB require pre-approval from a department head before the expense is incurred. Reimbursements are processed within 2 payroll cycles of submission.

SECTION 5: IT AND EQUIPMENT
Each employee is issued a laptop and standard peripherals upon joining. Equipment remains company property and must be returned upon resignation or termination. The office WiFi password is rotated quarterly; current credentials are available from the IT helpdesk. Lost or damaged equipment due to negligence may result in a deduction from final payroll, subject to a case-by-case review.

SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS AND PROMOTION
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Reviews assess performance against role-specific KPIs set at the start of each review cycle. Promotion decisions are made following the December review cycle and take effect the following February. Salary adjustments tied to performance are communicated within 2 weeks of the review meeting.

SECTION 7: RESIGNATION AND TERMINATION
Employees who wish to resign must provide written notice according to their contract, typically 30 days for confirmed employees. During the notice period, employees are expected to complete a handover of responsibilities. Final settlement, including any outstanding leave payout, is processed within 30 days of the last working day. The company reserves the right to terminate employment for cause, including breach of company policy, with immediate effect.
